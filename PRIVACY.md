# VeyLance privacy notice

VeyLance is a hackathon prototype for testing scoped access to a simulated federated learning workflow. It uses fictional hospitals and synthetic patient records. Do not enter real patient or other sensitive information into the prototype.

## Sign-in

The test application uses NamoID Hosted Auth. NamoID handles the sign-in flow and may process the email address and authentication information you provide there under its own privacy notice. VeyLance uses the resulting account identifier to recognize a signed-in user and assign application roles.

## Prototype records

When the application is operational, it is designed to record access requests, reviewer decisions, granted permissions, expiry and revocation events, and simulated training actions. Those records may include your account identifier, role, timestamps, and text you enter into request or review forms. Do not enter personal or confidential information in free-text fields. The published hospital dataset contains generated records only.

The application is still being built. This notice will be updated before the public demo if its data handling changes. The source repository does not contain sign-in credentials or user sessions.

## Contact

For questions about this prototype or its records, contact the maintainer through the [VeyLance repository](https://github.com/Yashika28284/Veylance). Do not post personal or medical information in a public GitHub issue.
